# Cemetery backdrop

`cemetery-twilight.webp` is a decorative fictional cemetery scene, generated with the built-in image-generation tool and encoded as WebP for this project.

Prompt: Create a wide, elegant atmospheric cemetery at twilight for a purple and pink municipal dashboard. A small classical stone mausoleum with columns, arched entrance and a simple cross stands in the lower center-left, framed by dark leafy trees, distant memorials, tiny crosses, and layered foliage. Warm rose light inside the entrance, lavender and plum shadows, dusty mauve sky, golden pink sunset on the right. Sophisticated painterly 3D illustration, peaceful and respectful. No text, logo, watermark, interface or people. Fictional decorative scene, not a factual photograph of Hinigaran.
